# Same-record reader-conditioned three-wave DURA fusion

A continuous IQ restorer combines the frozen DURA output, the aligned received waveform and the aligned output of the fixed source-trained spectral expert. The latter two are independently aligned to DURA with bounded delay and complex gain; unavailable edges are filled from the same DURA output. The network predicts two complex, bounded per-time coefficients for the raw-minus-DURA and expert-minus-DURA differences. Each real or imaginary coefficient is bounded by 0.35, and the sum can move beyond the range of a convex blend. Zero output initialization returns DURA exactly. Current-waveform F features and soft logits from frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL condition the coefficients; they are detached and are not identity ground truth. Full pipeline phase equivariance is not claimed because F features may depend on rotation. The frozen spectral expert is loaded with its exact source provenance and is checked unchanged.

Motivation is exploratory source pseudo-label complementarity between raw and DURA, not a claim that pseudo-labels are correct or that a usable selector exists. Train only on 8192 source fit IDs, fixed source views 21/22/23, five epochs, paired clean IQ, frozen F guidance and DURA-relative soft protection. The DURA model, both frozen F copies, blind estimator, gate and spectral expert remain fixed. Do not access transmitter identities, U_s truth, V, ground IQ or feedback for G training. At inference use only one received IQ and fixed weights; no clean target, scene, true identity, other record or model update.

Run a 64-ID real-source smoke and freeze 672 source predictions before L_s source scoring. Source results do not cancel the registered ground evaluation. Fix all seven Phase1 ground views of 168000 records before truth-last scoring and independent 119-group audit. Two CPU threads and established load limit apply, with all other experiments undisturbed. Previously explored simulations and F-derived pseudo targets are not independent blind or real-satellite evidence.
