# Phase1 DURA-aligned spectral waveform fusion

- Frozen original Phase1 DURA and frozen source-trained spectral-aligned checkpoint b326a21a54ae7b9b198e3d077c01a3919d8bd135 are the two waveform experts. The spectral expert uses legal source IQ and frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL guidance, reads no TX identity, U_s truth, V, ground/test, or target feedback. Its 8192 training IDs overlap the fusion gate's training IDs; 672 source evaluations use disjoint IDs but these records informed prior source diagnostics and are not an independent blind set.
- Each sample: compute both expert waveforms, fit +/-64 sample shift and one complex gain aligning the spectral output to the DURA output, then predict a bounded time-dependent signed coefficient from normalized received IQ, DURA, aligned expert and blind noise features. Output DURA + 0.5*tanh(gate)*(aligned expert - DURA); zero initialization gives DURA. Alignment and expert are fixed/no-gradient. The gate uses no target, clean reference, identity, true channel, scene or other sample at inference.
- Training uses paired permitted L_s/U_s clean and received IQ, frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL features/distribution/margin and soft DURA-relative aligned-IQ protection; F-guided. Same approved 8192 source fit IDs, three epochs 21/22/23 of views, five training epochs and receiver balancing as DURA context. Frozen DURA, reader, blind estimator, outer gate and spectral expert are checked unchanged, including expert state and no gradients.
- Source 20261103-phase1-dura-blendgate-source-s2026092701-r01: CPU two threads with load guard, actual source smoke 64 training IDs and 28 eval records, mandatory frozen nested expert count 1 and nonzero learned gate gradients. Complete 672 fixed source predictions before authorized L_s score.
- Ground 20261103-phase1-dura-blendgate-ground-test-s2026092701-r01: after source freeze replay and 168000 fixed Phase1 Main IDs each for clean and six practical views, frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL, independently score and audit only after all seven predictions fixed. Low source performance alone does not stop testing.
- Stop only on numeric, technical, process, source-data or frozen-component contract failure; preserve unrelated runs. Ground data are previously explored development data, not independent blind or real satellite measurements.
