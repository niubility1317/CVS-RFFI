# Phase1 DURA cross-view latent correction

The fixed DURA output is the anchor. A trainable correction observes the current raw and DURA IQ windows, their complex cross-correlation and lagged self-correlations, and the frozen blind-noise estimate. The representation is invariant to a common phase rotation of the raw and anchored IQ; the correction is equivariant under that simultaneous rotation. This property does not assert DURA itself is equivariant. Zero initialization returns DURA exactly. Inference uses only one current window and frozen components.

Train with three simulated views of each source record per update, paired clean IQ and frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL feature/distribution guidance. Add 0.1 times paired latent squared differences, 0.01 times the standard-deviation floor penalty, and 0.001 times the off-diagonal covariance penalty. These source-only coefficients are exploratory and fixed before evaluation. No DURA-relative waveform penalty is used. A noncollapsed latent may still encode signal content or nuisance rather than transmitter identity; consistency does not guarantee recognition improvement.

Use the established 8,192 source fit records, fixed five-epoch budget and frozen reader, DURA, blind estimator and gate. Do not access transmitter identities in training, unlabelled-source truth, validation truth, ground truth, target adaptation, or Phase3. Score 672 fixed source predictions truth-last, then produce all seven fixed predictions for 168,000 Phase1 ground records before independent truth scoring and 119-group audit. Continue the registered ground experiment regardless of source score. Report both relative 3% and three-percentage-point thresholds and the paired DURA comparison. Source and ground development data have been explored and are not independent blind evidence; channels are simulated.
