# Phase1 paired-view invariant latent recovery

The independent recovery network maps one received IQ window to one restored IQ window; it does not load DURA. It builds an invariant 64-dimensional representation from amplitudes and lagged complex correlations, conditions the waveform decoder on that representation and a frozen blind-noise estimate, and initializes exactly to its input. Its correction is globally phase equivariant. No information from other received windows is used at inference.

Training uses three simulated channel views of the same source recording per update. Alongside paired source-IQ and frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL feature and output guidance, it adds 0.1 times paired latent squared difference, 0.01 times the standard-deviation floor penalty, and 0.001 times the off-diagonal covariance penalty. These are exploratory fixed coefficients, not ground-tuned. The variance and covariance terms use the source training batch only. This objective does not guarantee that the latent captures transmitter identity rather than content or nuisance.

Use the established 8,192 source fit records, fixed five epochs, and frozen reader, blind estimator and gate. Do not access transmitter identity labels in training, unlabelled-source truth, validation-set truth, ground truth, target adaptation, or Phase3. Score 672 fixed source predictions truth-last. Produce all seven fixed predictions for 168,000 Phase1 ground records before the independent scorer reads truth; audit the established 119 groups. Continue this registered ground experiment irrespective of source score. Report both the relative 3% threshold and the three-percentage-point threshold, as well as comparison to DURA. Results from previously explored source and ground development data are not independent blind evidence; the channels are simulated.
