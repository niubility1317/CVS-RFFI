# Phase1 recurrent continuous DURA refinement

Starting from the frozen DURA waveform, apply the same trainable feedback correction three times. Every step receives the original received IQ, current corrected IQ and blind noise estimate. The condition is the frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL 160-dimensional feature and six soft logits computed once from the original DURA output for that same window; it is held fixed across steps, not recomputed from intermediate outputs. The correction weights are shared, and the output is continuous IQ, with no identity router or candidate selection. Zero initialization returns DURA exactly. The three steps can accumulate larger changes than one step and may amplify error. No claim of full-system phase equivariance is made because the frozen reader condition may itself change under rotation.

Train the final output end-to-end with the same source IQ alignment and frozen-reader feature/distribution objective as the single-step feedback model. The fixed condition is detached, and gradients flow through the recurrent IQ states and final frozen-reader loss. Use 8,192 source fit records and five epochs with DURA, reader, condition reader, blind estimator and gate frozen. Do not use transmitter identities in training, unlabelled-source truth, source V, ground truth, target adaptation, or Phase3. Inference uses the current IQ window only. Report the extra compute from three correction passes separately; latency is not assumed.

Fix all 672 source predictions before scoring. Continue the registered ground test regardless of source score: fix seven views for all 168,000 records, then independently score and audit 119 groups. Compare to both thresholds for relative 3% and three percentage points, and to the DURA baseline. Source/ground development data have been explored and channels are simulated rather than independent or real satellite evidence.
