\subsection{Strictly Supervised Architecture and Physical-Prior Controls}
The historical architecture comparison used the single-view SIG\_TIME
extractor with labeled-source cross entropy, 200 epochs (10,000 updates),
and eight model seeds. It used neither unlabeled IQ nor DURA. These results
are distinct from the final T1 recognizer, which uses three independently
learned SIG\_TIME views and the native semi-supervised schedule.

We additionally preregister a labeled-only architecture panel on the
five-source/seven-target F0 contract: 6,300 labeled training packets,
27,000 source-validation packets, six transmitters, and 168,000 target
packets. No unlabeled IQ is opened. Every new model uses the same
20+10+20-epoch schedule, 11,100 updates, labeled batch size 128, joint
default cosine classifier, and cross entropy with the default satellite
concatenation weight 0.68. Label smoothing, CRIC, RDrop, EMA, and
pseudo-labeling are disabled in this panel. The final native T1 method
remains fixed.

The panel adds eight settings with three F seeds each; the full X3 CE
reference reuses three corrected models. Three existing F20 models also
receive a strictly labeled-only system readout. All system readouts use
the same-contract DURA-L chain, including its labeled-only ancestors;
a restorer trained on unlabeled source IQ is excluded from the strict
supervision comparison. Variation over three F seeds is conditional on
one shared G seed, rather than three independent end-to-end replicas.

After freezing, source-only diagnostics select at most eight packets per
TX/RX/day stratum. Eleven fixed conditions examine flat gain and phase,
relative CFO, a delayed FIR echo, and energy-matched synthetic amplitude
nonlinearity. We record deployed-feature displacement, default-head
decision changes, relative-response displacement, and anchor-balanced
same-TX/cross-RX versus different-TX/same-RX distances within each day.
No probe is fitted and no target result controls training. Nonlinearity
interventions test sensitivity; they are not assumed to preserve an
unknown physical transmitter identity. These tests support an inductive
bias claim, not identification of hardware parameters or complete
receiver/channel disentanglement.

All raw and DURA-L predictions over the seven declared views must finish
before independent truth-side scoring. Accuracy, Macro-F1, per-RX/TX/day
results, clean damage, net corrections, paired seed differences, and
training/deployment cost will be retained, including negative results.
The new timing panel measures only the nine supervised structures on an
exclusive GPU; it does not repeat the completed uniform timing panel.

architecture checks. The 24 new F rows continue in the shared pipeline;
