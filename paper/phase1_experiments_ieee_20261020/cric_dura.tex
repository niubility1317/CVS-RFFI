\subsection{Complete PAMF: CRIC with DURA}
\label{sec:cric-dura}
The user-designated complete PAMF system applies the frozen DURA v1.0 restorer to each received IQ window, followed by CRIC and its original jointly trained cosine classifier. This includes clean observations; the system does not use the true channel scene to decide whether restoration is applied. CRIC denotes Cross-Receiver Identity Contrastive Learning. The final identification recipe is T1-E50: scratch20 (13 labeled-only and 7 joint epochs), own10 joint epochs, and own20 joint epochs. Student and own EMA states are strictly inherited, while AdamW, the segment cosine schedule and temporal state reset. The total is 13 labeled-only and 37 joint epochs, with labeled supervision retained throughout.

The final T1 representation retains SIG\_TIME\_X3, time/frequency/PA fusion, reference response, unit-aware centered Sinc optimization and its jointly trained default cosine head. Each labeled batch uses three actual R-Drop pairs: ordinary128, mixed128 (ordinary64 plus As64), and raw128. The supervised objective is $0.5L_{RD,ord128}+0.25L_{RD,ord64}+0.25L_{CE,As64}+0.25L_{RD,raw128}$; As KL is zero, ordinary/raw KL is one and label smoothing is 0.01. The total CE dose is 1.25. As is generated from raw source IQ before ordinary augmentation. CRIC uses only the ordinary128 shallow/intermediate maps, averaged over the two ordinary forwards; temperature is 0.2, with cumulative warm5/ramp10 to weight0.1. Native T1 retains its ownEMA and original unlabeled gates, PL weight0.16 and entropy0.01 as the explicitly fixed final-method exception.

DURA uses two learned-prior stages and two conjugate-gradient updates per stage. Its 266951 unique parameters predict a coarse restoration and a current-window real-linear action, with a main and conjugate-image response. The fixed runtime uses residual reuse and CUDA Graphs in FP32 with TF32 disabled. Its 27 FFT/IFFT calls describe G only. Restorer training uses source IQ and simulated pairs without TX labels, identity-model weights/outputs, or target observations. All training ancestors are subject to the same source contract.

\subsubsection{Latency Boundary and Repetition}
We measure F alone, G alone, and the actual online G+F call. Cached restored-IQ reads never replace G in a complete-system timing. Module profiles are separate diagnostics; their medians are not added to synthesize end-to-end latency.

The common benchmark uses one exclusive RTX3090, the same Python3.10/Torch2.1 stack, two CPU threads, FP32 and both TF32 flags disabled. Existing Torch1.11 comparator costs are not ranked directly against DURA's Torch2.1 timing. External comparison models use eager calls; F and G/GF additionally report the declared Graph routes. This fixed seed01 timing panel separates execution-route effects from the three-seed accuracy experiments. Healthy training is not interrupted to obtain a timing GPU.

Batches are 1/8/32/64/128. Five balanced-order rounds use 100 warmups and 1000 measured calls per round for B1, or 20 warmups and 100 measured calls for larger batches. We report synchronized median/P95, sample counts, between-round variation, whole-batch latency, throughput, resident/incremental peak memory, and first-call setup.
