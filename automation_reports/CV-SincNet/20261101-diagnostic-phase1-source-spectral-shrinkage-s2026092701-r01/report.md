# Phase1 source adaptive cycle-spectral shrinkage network

The source-only network modifies DURA by learning sample-dependent attenuation of 12 singular components in the phase-demodulated 20-sample cycle residual. It preserves the periodic mean and the final 16 samples. Its 14 inputs are normalized singular values, a blind noise proxy and an input/output difference ratio; a fixed gate restricts modification to estimated low-SNR signals. Frozen Tri-SIG-TIME SSL and DURA remain the ground backbone and restorer. The source-only training loss combines IQ reconstruction to paired clean IQ aligned within ±16 samples, features and soft probabilities of the unaligned clean ground signal from the frozen teacher, and a shrinkage penalty. No target test or U_s true labels are used. Inference uses received IQ and fixed model outputs only.

The protocol was frozen before full training. Among 2,434 sampled source IDs, 1,622 earlier-day records were training, 547 receiver 4/6 day-3 records validation and 265 receiver 8 day-3 holdout; 1,768 gated impaired training views were used. The validation rule selected epoch 5 blend 0.25, aggregate +4 across impaired views. On 260 valid source holdout targets, low-urban correctness remained 232 (two repairs and two damages), while true L_s improved 65/74 to 67/74; mid urban improved 242 to 244 (L_s 69/74 unchanged), low suburban 253 unchanged and clean 260 unchanged. On all 265 paired IQs, low-urban aligned mean IQ MSE within ±64 shifts on the central 128 samples rose from 0.075706 to 0.075801.

A separately executed fixed-model replay replaced clean training targets by zeros and omitted clean-teacher target features during inference. All heldout classification counts and IQ metrics matched exactly, confirming the candidate computation does not depend on those paired-clean targets. The complete training/selection experiment was independently repeated with identical result JSON.

A further fixed-model source evaluation was defined before execution using seed 2026110541; all sampled physical IDs of both the model-development sample (2026110531) and previous low-rank sample (2026110521) were excluded. They may overlap other earlier diagnostics. The same frozen experts have seen source records. With new channel/hardware seeds, receiver-8 day-3 low urban increased from 238 to 239 of 259 valid mixed targets; true L_s stayed 60/67. All other examined views on this receiver were unchanged. Across 269 paired IQs, its mean low-urban ±64 aligned MSE rose from 0.081640 to 0.081997. Receiver 4/6 day-3 had low urban 444 to 446 of 514 and true L_s 103/121 unchanged. The independent full fresh evaluation reproduced identical JSON.

These small source-only differences are not a demonstrated target improvement. Simulated satellite signals have not been validated as real satellite; selection and exploratory source samples may share context with earlier studies. None of these results establishes the six-scene Phase1 ≤3% goal.
