# Phase1 source low-rank cycle residual pilot

Frozen DURA output is phase-demodulated, split into twelve cycles of 20 IQ samples, and decomposed into its per-sample cycle mean plus nonperiodic residual. Rank-4 or rank-8 approximation affects only that residual, with fixed blends 0.25, 0.5 or 1; the final 16 samples are preserved. An existing blind SNR proxy and normalized input/output difference gate control modification. Inputs at inference are the received waveform and frozen models only. The 20-sample structure and gate came from earlier source exploration.

On 557 source validation records from receivers 4/6, rank 4 blend 1 was selected with aggregate +6 correct across the three impaired views. On receiver 8/day 3 holdout, low urban changed 221 to 220 of 256 valid mixed targets (9 repairs, 10 damages; true L_s 70/81 unchanged), mid urban 236 to 237, low suburban 250 to 249, and clean 256 unchanged. The mean low-urban paired IQ error after ±16-sample alignment increased from 0.095197 to 0.107769 across all 265 holdout IQ samples. The complete experiment was repeated and JSON matched.

A posthoc wider ±64-sample alignment on the central 128 samples, with synthetic ±61-sample-shift tests, gives low-urban mean IQ error 0.073832 to 0.085484. None of the nine classification repairs by rank 4 blend 1 improved paired IQ error. A weaker rank 4 blend 0.25 improves mean IQ error to 0.073225 but produces no classification repairs and has 219 correct. If per-record truth can choose among DURA and six fixed candidates, correctness could be 230/256, or 223/256 if each repair must additionally reduce wide aligned IQ error; these label-assisted and paired-IQ-assisted oracles cannot be deployed and are not bounds on arbitrary methods.

This is source-only exploration with true L_s and confident U_s clean-teacher pseudo targets. Source samples may overlap other diagnostics, and frozen models saw source records. There were zero U_s true label, validation IQ or test IQ reads; simulator is not validated real satellite data. No target test was run.
